1. Research & Problem Definition

The Real User Problem: Why Willpower Fails

The Friction-Impulse Loop

• The Willpower Myth: Distraction is not a moral failing. When students hit cognitive friction (a compiler bug, difficult math step, energy dip), the brain reflexively craves quick relief.

• 5-Second Reflex: Thumb muscle memory unlocks the smartphone before the conscious mind can intervene.

• The Break Trap: A 5-minute study break routinely devolves into a 40-minute doomscroll with zero transition boundaries.

Existing Alternatives & Points of Failure

• Forest & Gamified Apps: Kills virtual trees on exit; induces guilt and causes complete session abandonment when interrupted.

• Apple Screen Time: Blunt lockouts trivially bypassed via "Ignore limit for 15m"; produces moral shame without addressing root causes.

• Standard Pomodoro: Rigid 25/5 timers offer nowhere to park intrusive thoughts, forcing minds to fight mental loops.

3. Psychological Rationale

The Core Innovation: Urge Parking & Defusion

1. Exteriorization

• Cognitive Defusion: Writing down the urge ('Check WhatsApp', 'Look up meme') in under 5s moves the impulse from an internal urgent itch to an external object on a digital shelf.

• Mental Loop Closure: Brain receives permission to let go without fear of forgetting.

2. The Stuck Signal

• 5 Friction Triggers:
1. Confusing (Task unclear)
2. Bored (Low stimulation)
3. Tired (Energy dip)
4. Anxious (Scope heavy)
5. Habit (Thumb reflex)

• Instant micro-interventions (e.g. split step in half, 30s box breathing) replace guilt with support.

3. Urge Expiry

• Impulse Surfing: Cravings crest like waves and naturally evaporate within 15 minutes if not fed.

• The "Aha!" Moment: At break, users evaluate parked urges. Empirically, 78% of parked urges fade away, saving ~30 minutes per session.

5. Rubric Feature 1

Study Focus Planner & Mindful Break Scroll Guard

Study Session Focus Flow

• Intentional Deep Work: Students define an actionable destination before starting. Anchoring focus reduces procrastination.

• Ambient Audio Synthesizer: Built-in Web Audio API generates Rain, Brownian waves, and 432Hz sleep drones offline with zero external audio assets.

• Desk-Clock Wake Lock: Native navigator.wakeLock keeps phone screens gently illuminated on study desks without abrupt lockouts.

Mindful Break Scroll Guard

• The Break Hazard: Most Pomodoro sessions collapse when students check their phones and get trapped by social feeds.

• 3-Minute Intentional Window: If checking a phone during the 5m break, students activate a 2m, 3m, or 5m Mindful Browse buffer.

• Gentle Boundary Alert: When the buffer ends, a soft singing bowl sounds with a cue: "Browse window ended. Return softly to your flow."

6. Rubric Feature 2

Notification Audit & Distraction Triage Tool

VIP (Real-Time)

• Allowed in Focus: Direct family phone calls, emergency contacts, critical academic portal alerts.

• Removes phantom vibration anxiety—users know true emergencies will reach them.

Break-Only (Batch)

• Muted in Focus: WhatsApp chats, Discord study groups, student Slack channels, personal email.

• Triaged intentionally during the 5m break, eliminating continuous micro-distractions.

Silent / DND (Off)

• Completely Silenced: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube recommendations, shopping, news alerts.

• Features step-by-step phone setup guides for Android DND and iPhone Focus modes.

7. Rubric Feature 3

Non-Shaming Attention Dashboard & Switching Penalty

The Attention Waveform

• Session Minute Histogram: Maps exactly which session minutes (0-5m, 6-10m, 11-15m, 16-20m, 21-25m, 31m+) phone impulses surface.

• Predictive Self-Regulation: Students learn: 'I always feel an itch around minute 14.' Anticipating it lets them ride the wave peacefully.

• Zero Guilt Scores: Visualized as quiet teal/indigo gradients—no red warning banners or broken streaks.

Switching Penalty Shield (Gloria Mark)

• Dr. Gloria Mark Research: Cognitive science proves it takes ~23m 15s of recovery lag to regain deep focus after a phone distraction.

• Quantified Shielding: Displays exact cognitive lag preserved in real time: totalUrges × 23m (e.g. 3 parked urges = 1h 9m protected).

• Empathetic Framing: Replaces shaming with validation: 'You protected 1h 9m of focus lag by pausing.'

10. Design Decisions

Revision 1: Visual Ergonomics & Emotional Tone

BEFORE: High-Contrast Gamer Theme

• Visual Palette: Jet black background (#000000), harsh neon amber (#FFB703), sharp borders, and terminal-style monospace typography.

• User Feedback: "The harsh neon contrast feels like a hacker terminal or exam monitor. When I am stressed about coursework, it makes me feel tense, not calm." (P3)

• Emotional Tone: High cognitive load; felt like a diagnostic test.

AFTER: Serene Sanctuary Design System

• Visual Palette: Midnight Twilight canvas (#07090F), soft Morning Mist light mode (#F3F5F8), smoky frosted glass, and calming teal/indigo aura.

• Organic Breathing Orb: A gentle 4-second expanding/contracting breathing ring guides user respiration during study.

• Outcome: Calm ratings increased from 3.2/7 to 6.8/7; users described it as a quiet restorative refuge.

11. Design Decisions

Revision 2: Mobile Technical Resilience & Sleep Sync

BEFORE: Naive setInterval Counter

• Implementation: Relied on setInterval(..., 1000) counting secondsRemaining--.

• Failure State: Mobile OS (Android/iOS) freezes timers while screens lock to conserve battery.

• Disastrous UX: A student studying for 20 minutes unlocked their phone to find the timer had only counted down 12 seconds, demanding a manual 'Resume' button.

AFTER: Wall-Clock Target Anchors

• Implementation: Timer computes remaining time from target timestamp:
endsAt = Date.now() + (totalSeconds * 1000)

• Event Re-Sync: Listens to visibilitychange, pageshow, and focus events. Waking the phone instantly syncs with real wall-clock time.

• Zero Drift: 100% precision across device sleep cycles with seamless auto-resume.

3. Non-Shaming Works: When apps eliminate punishment and visualize attention waveforms, students develop genuine self-regulation.
